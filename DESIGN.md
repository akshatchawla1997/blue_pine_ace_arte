## Brand & Style

This design system expresses ultra-prime residential real estate, balancing the institutional advisory pedigree of Blue Pine Consulting with the high-concept architectural allure of ACE Arte in Sector 150, Noida. 

The aesthetic is **Modern Architectural Luxury** fused with crisp **Haute Editorial Minimalism**. It avoids disposable broker cliches in favor of museum-grade layout discipline, deliberate negative space, gold hairline framing, and authoritative typography. The UI commands high trust for affluent investors, NRI buyers, and discerning high-net-worth homeowners seeking iconic residences.

Visual motifs prioritize precision:
- High-contrast, deeply saturated royal midnight blues establish fiduciary stability.
- Polished brushed champagne-gold accents serve strictly as precision accents, dividers, and critical conversion triggers.
- Monolithic card blocks and whisper-thin hairline borders invoke fine European architectural elevations.
- Imagery is treated with warm tonal depth, layered against stark alabaster surfaces and nocturnal contrast zones.

## Colors

The chromatic hierarchy establishes immediate gravitas.

- **Primary (`#0B3576`)**: Blue Pine's signature royal navy. Used for commanding structural headers, primary hero backings, luxury section anchors, and dominant interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted champagne gold. Represents architectural value, RERA badges, luxury tags, price reveal triggers, and hairline division lines.
- **Tertiary (`#061A3A`)**: Deep abyss navy. Utilized for deep-contrast feature panels, VIP inquiry modal backgrounds, and the night-mode footer.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`)**: Crisp alabaster slate. Prevents stark clinical whiteout while maintaining pristine luminosity across specifications and layout showcases.

### Contextual Usage
- Dark sections (Hero, VIP booking, Location Matrix) utilize `surface-dark` (`#040D1B`) with `gold-core` borders and typography in pure white.
- Light sections (Floor plans, amenities, unit layouts) utilize `surface-alabaster` with `text-primary-dark` copy and `brand-navy-royal` structural cards.
- Trust marks, including RERA badge registration (`UPRERAPRJ528653/07/2026`), employ a subtle gold hairline outline over deep navy to reinforce regulatory compliance and investor confidence.

## Typography

The type system blends classical high-editorial authority with modern digital legibility.

- **Display & Section Headers (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs classical proportions, high contrast stems, and refined serifs. Headings deliberately feature two-tone treatment—pairing an unadorned serif weight with an italicized or gold-highlighted accent phrase (e.g., *Sector 150 Noida* **Price List**).
- **Body & Data Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers pristine legibility across property technical specifications, square-footage breakdowns, floor plan dimensions, and fast mobile inputs.
- **Eyebrow Badges & Micro-Labels (`label-caps`)**: Always set uppercase with generous `0.15em` tracking to emulate European architectural blueprints and luxury catalog indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a **12-column architectural grid** characterized by expansive vertical pacing and crisp, mathematical alignments.

- **Rhythm**: Page sections breathe with substantial vertical padding (`space-xl` to `5rem`), preventing information overload and creating an unhurried, luxury salon experience.
- **Container Structure**: Max-width is constrained to `1280px` for optimal readability and editorial layout, centered within the viewport.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; external margin `1rem`. Dual-pricing tables condense into stacked elevation cards. Bottom conversion actions dock into a fixed persistent contact bar.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1024px)`: 8-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; external margin `2rem`. Specification matrices collapse to 2 columns.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px)`: 12-column layout; gutter `2rem`; external margin `4rem`. Asymmetric multi-column spreads for architectural showcases, floor plan tabs, and split-pane inquiry layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, unrefined drop shadows, relying instead on **Atmospheric Layering** and **Hairline Precision**:

- **Hairline Framing**: Separation is primarily established by single-pixel gold or cool-slate rules: `border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` on dark tiers and `border: 1px solid rgba(11, 53, 118, 0.1)` on light tiers.
- **Nocturnal Backdrops**: High-priority modules (Hero lead form, VIP site visit booking) sit on deep navy backgrounds layered with soft multi-stop radial highlights (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(14, 72, 158, 0.25), transparent 75%)`).
- **Elevated Spec Cards**: Floating pricing cards and floor plan tiles feature an ultra-soft diffused ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(11, 53, 118, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphism in Navigation & Overlays**: Sticky headers and floating mobile trigger bars use backdrop filters: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. In nocturnal segments, `background: rgba(6, 26, 58, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft-tailored architectural geometry** (`roundedness: 1`).

- Core UI elements (buttons, inquiry form inputs, property cards, badge tags) feature a delicate `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This conveys structural permanence and refined poise, avoiding both the clinical harshness of completely sharp edges and the casual tech-consumer feel of heavy pill radii.
- Pill treatment (`rounded: 9999px`) is reserved exclusively for the floating WhatsApp quick-action bubble and compact status chips (e.g., `RERA Registered`).

## Components

### 1. Primary Buttons & CTAs
- **Gold Royal Accent (Primary conversion)**: Background `brand-navy-royal`, border `1px solid gold-core`, text in crisp white or gold, uppercase tracked `label-caps`. Hover states gently shift to `gold-core` with deep navy typography.
- **VIP Lead Trigger ("Price on Request")**: Gold gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #DFB76C 0%, #C5A059 100%)`), text in deep navy, subtle gold sheen on hover.
- **WhatsApp Consultation Trigger**: Dedicated floating and inline button featuring `whatsapp-green` accent with clean white iconography.

### 2. Badges & Micro Trust Marks
- **RERA Official Badge**: Deep navy fill, 1px gold hairline frame, subtle lock/verification icon, displaying `UPRERAPRJ528653/07/2026`.
- **Typology Chips**: 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and Penthouse tags styled with alabaster surfaces, slate borders, and navy uppercase typography.

### 3. Specification & Pricing Matrix
- **Dual Display Model**:
  - *Desktop*: Sleek data grid with alternating alabaster and white rows, separated by hairline gold rules. Columns delineate `Unit Type`, `Super Area`, `Balcony Deck`, and actionable `Price on Request` callout buttons.
  - *Mobile*: Modular stacked cards with clear horizontal dividers and prominent primary CTAs.

### 4. VIP Lead Capture Forms
- **Input Fields**: Crisp alabaster or pristine white backgrounds, slate border (`#CBD5E1`), transitioning to an active 1.5px `gold-core` focus ring without heavy outer glow. Labels set in tracked uppercase.
- **Floating Conversion Anchor**: A mobile-responsive bottom dock hosting telephone direct-dial (`91-9560200299`), WhatsApp consultation, and instant brochure download modal triggers.

### 5. Floor Plan Layout Showcases
- High-contrast visual containment, interactive tab controls toggling unit sizes (e.g., 1950 sq.ft to 3100 sq.ft), integrated download overlays with watermark protections, and 1-click booking inquiry drawers.
